# LATTICE STRIKE — field guide

## Your first minute

1. Choose **Lattice Strike** for team territory combat, or **Operations** for
   co-op against the Director. Quick start opens the field briefing first.
2. Leave your HQ and follow the supply link to your **front gate**. Stand in
   its capture ring and clear enemies. **Capture is automatic.**
3. Push a connected relay or side siphon. You can only capture beside a node
   your team owns. The HUD suggests a legal objective and gives its distance.
4. Keep the link home defended. Only nodes connected to HQ earn team **FLUX**.
   Capturing far forward is less valuable if the enemy cuts your supply line.

In PvP, hold a majority long enough to win by dominance; objective score decides
at the time limit. In Operations, clear five waves before time runs out and keep
the HQ alive. Kills help you take and defend ground; they are not the win condition.

## Read the battlefield

| Symbol | Structure | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ⌂ | HQ | Protect your supply origin; respond to the Operations siege alarm. |
| ▲ | Front gate | Capture first, then defend the link to the shared objectives. |
| ⬢ | Relay | Contest the central junction and rotate to the weaker side. |
| ◆ | Siphon | Establish a side front and connected income. |
| ⇢ | Traversal anchor | Read the interaction prompt before pressing the key. |
| ▣ | Depot | Hold the apron to capture; collect a loaner once available. |

Open **Supply map** in the HUD for the node graph. Solid links join your team's
owned nodes; dashed links show potential connections. The white dot is you and
the outlined node is your suggested objective. This is a topology diagram, not
a turn-by-turn pathfinder. Ownership is also written in words.

## Controls

These are defaults; the briefing and HUD reflect your remapped bindings.

| Action | Default |
|---|---|
| Harness ability | `Q` |
| Operator mobility | `X` |
| Ride / cut / lock / repair / terminal / enter vehicle | `E`, according to prompt |
| Arm SCAN / GO-HOLD / ATTACK | `N` / `M` / `P` |
| Choose an order target | Number shown beside the target |
| Issue armed order | `Enter` |
| Cancel armed order | `Escape` |
| Peek command board | Hold `B`; release to return to combat |

Orders work in local and network play. When no order is armed, number keys still
select weapons and Enter still opens network chat. SCAN spends team FLUX.

### Traversal and climbing

Walk onto a device anchor and wait for **RIDE** before pressing Interact. Standing
in the outer interaction ring can offer **CUT** or **LOCK**, denying the route to
both teams. A disabled device offers **REPAIR**. Channels can be interrupted;
watch the progress and defend the operator doing the work.

Grapple operators aim at a solid surface above them and **hold Mobility** to reel
upward. A nearby clear lip permits a mantle onto the roof. Release to detach.
The hook reaches 14 m; low wall hits, ceilings and overhangs do not grant passage
through geometry. Carrier restrictions still apply. Ground ramps serve loadouts
without a grapple and players waiting on cooldowns.

## Choose a field role

Your existing combat ability, signature and mobility verb remain important.
LATTICE adds these objective-specific tools; their full conditions and limits
appear on the loadout screen and under **Your field role** in the HUD.

| Operator | Objective contribution |
|---|---|
| ChatGPT | During its post-swap window, share actual ammunition with a nearby ally. |
| Claude | Hold an owned point to cleanse a nearby ally's slow. |
| Grok | Maintain Heat to accelerate capture. |
| Meta | Crouch and brace to channel repairs on a cut owned point. |
| Gemini | Weapon swaps open a short faster-capture window. |
| DeepSeek | Stationary overwatch spots a visible opponent. |
| Mistral | Keep moving inside the point for faster capture. |
| Kimi | Moving reconnaissance spots a visible opponent at longer range. |
| Qwen | Faster capture, terminal channels and economy-node PRIME. |

| Harness | Additional effect when its ability is accepted |
|---|---|
| OpenClaw | Disrupt hostile capture progress on a nearby point. |
| Hermes | At a connected owned siphon, trade personal REQ for team FLUX. |
| OpenCode | Share ammunition with nearby allies on the front. |
| Claude Code | Cleanse slow from nearby allies. |
| Codex | Repair an owned uncontested cut link and its sabotaged terminal. |
| Cline | Dash opens a short faster-capture window. |
| Roo Code | Ward an owned point to slow hostile capture. |

Bonuses are bounded: capture uses the strongest contribution rather than adding
every bonus, supply transfers ammunition rather than creating it, and shared
cooldowns prevent a stack of identical support effects. Reconnaissance marks
provide information, not SCAN's damage bonus.

## Operations rhythm

- **During a wave:** hold connected ground, clear the wave, and watch the HQ
  health and siege warning. Fall back early enough to stop a breach.
- **Between waves:** use the spend window to fortify, repair, resupply or
  reinforce. FLUX belongs to the team; REQUISITION belongs to the player.
- **At terminals:** read HACK / DEPLOY / VAULT, press Interact, and protect the
  channel. Role and ownership requirements can make a terminal unavailable.
- Reopen the briefing from the pause menu or **Settings → Help → Lattice**.

For a first match, Quick Start fills a 4v4 PvP practice roster or gives your
Operations squad three bot teammates. Custom Match Setup keeps your chosen
roster, time limit and modifiers.
